[1m[33m10.1 Reading a File[0m
[90m-------------------[0m
[32m    content = File.read("data.txt")[0m
[32m    puts content[0m

Read line by line:

[32m    end[0m

[1m[33m10.2 Writing a File[0m
[90m-------------------[0m
Overwrite the whole file:

[32m    File.write("out.txt", "Hello Ruby")[0m

Append to the end:

[32m    File.open("out.txt", "a") do |f|[0m
[32m      f.puts "Another line"[0m
[32m    end[0m

[1m[33m10.3 The Safe open-with-block Pattern[0m
[90m-------------------------------------[0m
The block form closes the file for you:

[32m    File.open("log.txt", "a") do |f|[0m
[32m      f.puts "logged event"[0m
[32m    end[0m

Without a block you must close manually:

[32m    f = File.open("log.txt", "w")[0m
[32m    f.puts "data"[0m
[32m    f.close[0m

[1m[33m10.4 File Modes[0m
[90m---------------[0m
[32m    "r"   read (default)[0m
[32m    "w"   write, truncating[0m
[32m    "a"   append[0m
[32m    "r+"  read/write[0m
[32m    "w+"  read/write, truncating[0m
[32m    "b"   binary, combine with another mode ("rb")[0m
